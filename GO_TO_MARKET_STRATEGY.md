# Survivor Exchange - Go-To-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

**Product**: Survivor Exchange - Dedicated auction marketplace for Loot Survivor beast NFTs on Starknet

**Current State**: Beta live, sellers listing, need buyer liquidity

**Goal**: Become THE destination for Loot Survivor beast trading

**Timeline**: 12-week launch plan

---

## 1. Target Audience

### Primary: Active Loot Survivor Players

| Segment | Description | Where to Find | What They Want |
|---------|-------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Grinders** | Play daily, accumulate beasts | Discord, Twitter, in-game leaderboards | Sell excess beasts, buy upgrades |
| **Collectors** | Want specific beasts (T1, shiny, animated) | NFT communities, Realms Discord | Find rare beasts, complete sets |
| **Flippers** | Buy low, sell high | Trading Discords, CT (Crypto Twitter) | Price inefficiencies, volume |
| **Summit Chasers** | Compete for Summit leaderboard | Summit leaderboard, competitive channels | Best beasts for climbing |

### Secondary: Starknet Ecosystem

| Segment | Description | Opportunity |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Starknet degens | Try new dApps, farm activity | May discover LS through your platform |
| Gaming investors | Back on-chain gaming projects | Awareness, potential partnerships |

### NOT Your Target (Don't Waste Time Here)

- General NFT collectors (don't know Loot Survivor)
- Ethereum-only users (not on Starknet)
- Non-gamers looking for "investment" NFTs

## 2. Value Proposition

### For Sellers
> "Stop guessing prices. Let buyers compete for your beasts."

- Auction format = price discovery
- Bundle sales (up to 163 beasts)
- Multi-token payments
- No more stale fixed-price listings

### For Buyers
> "Find undervalued beasts. Snipe auctions. Build your collection."

- Filter by exactly what you want
- Summit badges show elite beasts
- Bid what YOU think it's worth
- Direct offers to sellers

### vs. Alternatives

| Platform | Weakness | Your Advantage |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| General NFT marketplaces | No LS-specific features | Summit badges, beast filters, LS focus |
| Discord trading | Risky, no escrow | Secure smart contracts, transparent |
| Direct trades | Trust issues, manual | Automated, trustless |

## 8. Metrics & Success Criteria

### North Star Metric
**Monthly Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV)** - Total value of beasts traded

### Weekly Tracking

| Metric | Week 1-2 Target | Week 3-4 Target | Week 5-8 Target |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Total sales | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| Unique sellers | 5 | 15 | 40 |
| Unique buyers | 5 | 20 | 60 |
| GMV | $500 | $2,500 | $10,000 |
| Twitter followers | 100 | 300 | 750 |

### Health Metrics

- **Sell-through rate**: % of auctions that get bids
- **Repeat users**: % of users who transact 2+ times
- **Time to first bid**: How long auctions sit before bids

### Red Flags to Watch

- Auctions consistently expiring with no bids → Need more buyers
- Same few sellers, no new ones → Need seller acquisition
- Lots of visits, no transactions → UX or trust issue
